# STARZ Moderation Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026.

STARZ Moderation Bot is operated by the STARZ bot owner for the STARZ Discord and Rust Console Edition community. This policy explains how the bot handles information while providing community help, welcomes, and staff-reviewed evidence features.

## Information used

The bot uses Discord account identifiers, display information, and member-join events to post configured welcomes. For automatic help, it reads message text in accessible supported channels to match configured directory topics. It stores server, channel, role, and message identifiers; configuration; help and sticky text; and uploaded panel media.

The bot also processes trusted ended-stream notifications, retaining extracted stream facts and source references, and stores creator information, submissions, evidence, and staff-review records provided through its creator workflows. Where configured, the local phrase feature may store original and translated phrases; this does not involve an external AI service.

The bot does not collect Discord online-status or presence information and does not keep a general archive of all server conversations.

## Purpose and access

Information is used to run the bot's configured community features, provide support, review submitted evidence, and diagnose operational problems. The bot does not use message content to train AI or machine-learning models.

Authorized staff and the owner can access records needed to operate these features. Messages or results posted in Discord are visible according to the destination channel's permissions.

## Storage and retention

Operational records are stored outside Discord on Railway-hosted storage, which provides storage-layer encryption. Staff functions are protected by configured Discord roles and permissions. Discord and Railway process information to provide their platform and hosting services.

Some temporary and creator-evidence records are automatically purged, but there is no single automatic deletion period for all data. Configuration, stream records, uploaded utility media, and some audit or backup records can remain until manually removed. Leaving the server or stopping use of the bot does not automatically erase all stored records.

## Deletion requests and choices

Open a support ticket in the STARZ Discord server and ask to contact the bot owner to request deletion or discuss privacy concerns. Identify the bot and the records concerned. Requests are handled manually; the owner may need to verify the relevant account. No fixed response or deletion deadline is currently promised.

Removing data needed by a feature can prevent that feature from working until the necessary information is supplied again. This does not automatically ban a user from the bot; later use can create new records.

The bot has no universal per-user switch to opt out of all message processing. Server administrators can restrict channel access and disable supported features, and users can avoid optional submissions.

## Updates

This policy will be updated when the bot's data practices change. For questions, contact the bot owner through a STARZ support ticket.
